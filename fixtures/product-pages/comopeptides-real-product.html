The CJC-1295/Ipamorelin blend combines two complementary research peptides:

  • CJC-1295 (no DAC): A 29–amino-acid synthetic analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone.

  • Ipamorelin: A five–amino-acid synthetic peptide that acts as a highly selective agonist of the ghrelin/growth hormone secretagogue receptor (GHS-R1a).

Together, the precise receptor affinity and minimal off-target activity make it an ideal research reagent for examining ghrelin receptor signaling pathways, ligand–receptor kinetics, and downstream intracellular responses in cellular and in vivo models.

Note: The CJC-1295/Ipamorelin blend is intended for research purposes only and is not approved for human use or consumption. All products are shipped in lyophilized form and must be reconstituted to a liquid for research and testing.  We are unable to provide any dosing instructions.
