Reconstitution Solution, is a sterile, non-pyrogenic solution containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a preservative. This preservative helps inhibit bacterial growth, ensuring the solution remains stable and safe for laboratory use over multiple applications. Reconstitution Solution is widely utilized in research environments for reconstituting peptides, proteins, and other lyophilized compounds. Its preservative properties make it an essential tool for precise, contamination-free experiments, supporting reliable and reproducible results.

Note: Reconstitution Solution is intended for research and laboratory purposes only and is not for human use or consumption.

How should this be stored?
Lyophilized (powder) form: freezer at −20°C for long-term, or fridge (2–8°C) for short-term. Once reconstituted: refrigerate (2–8°C), use within 30–60 days depending on the peptide. Avoid freeze-thaw cycles. Keep out of direct sunlight.
What’s the shelf life?
Lyophilized: 24+ months at −20°C. Reconstituted: 30–60 days refrigerated, depending on the specific peptide.
